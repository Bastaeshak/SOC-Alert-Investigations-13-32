# CLD-0227: Reported email: genuine Bexley statement of work

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 7 · 2026-09-02
Verdict: False positive · Severity (my rating): Informational (reported Low)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
hr-manager@cloudora.io reported an email received at 07:55 UTC on Oct 13 from e.walsh@bexleypay.example with the subject “Signed SOW and revised schedule” and a PDF attachment. Because Eleanor Walsh's environment was involved in the ongoing Bexley compromise, the message was investigated to determine whether it represented continued attacker activity or legitimate business communication.

## Hypothesis
Malicious hypothesis: The attacker retained access to Eleanor's compromised mailbox and used it to send another malicious message to Cloudora.

Benign hypothesis: Eleanor legitimately sent the signed SOW and revised schedule as part of normal Bexley business operations following the disruption.

## Evidence checked
Evidence Checked

The raw email was reviewed and showed:

From: e.walsh@bexleypay.example
Return-Path: e.walsh@bexleypay.example
Received from established Bexley infrastructure: mail.bexleypay.example (198.51.100.90)
Received at 07:55:41 UTC Oct 13
Recipient: hr-manager@cloudora.io
Subject: Signed SOW and revised schedule
SPF: Pass
DKIM: Pass
DMARC: Pass
Message content was consistent with the Bexley business relationship and revised go-live schedule.
Bexley_SOW_signed.pdf was reported as having no active content.
No malicious link or other malicious content was identified in the provided message.

Because Eleanor's environment was previously compromised, additional checks were performed rather than relying only on the authenticated email.

KQL 1 — Check for account containment/reset evidence
CloudoraAudit_CL
| where TimeGenerated between (datetime(2026-10-12 00:00:00) .. datetime(2026-10-13 07:55:41))
| where TargetUser =~ "ewalsh.ext@cloudora.io"
   or InitiatedBy =~ "ewalsh.ext@cloudora.io"
| project TimeGenerated, ActivityDisplayName, InitiatedBy, TargetUser, IPAddress, Result, Details
| order by TimeGenerated asc

Purpose: Determine whether the available audit telemetry documented a password reset, session/token revocation, or other containment action involving Eleanor before the reported email.

Result: No rows returned. The available audit telemetry does not confirm that Eleanor's account was reset or sessions were revoked before the email.

KQL 2 — Review Eleanor's surrounding sign-in activity
signin
| where Timestamp between (datetime(2026-10-12 00:00:00) .. datetime(2026-10-13 12:00:00))
| where User =~ "ewalsh.ext@cloudora.io"
| project Timestamp, User, IP, Location, Client, Device, MFA, Result, Notes
| order by Timestamp asc

Purpose: Identify successful or suspicious account activity surrounding the reported email.

Result: One sign-in attempt occurred at 04:44:09 UTC Oct 13 from known malicious infrastructure 192.0.2.203. Conditional Access blocked the attempt under the active incident block. No successful Eleanor sign-in was identified in the searched window.

Week 2 helpdesk records were also reviewed. No record confirming Eleanor's password reset or session revocation was identified.

## Action
No malicious content was identified in the reported email, so no immediate email containment is required.

However, because Eleanor's mailbox/workstation was previously compromised and the available evidence does not confirm that her account was reset or sessions revoked, follow up with Eleanor/Bexley through a trusted communication channel to:

Confirm that Eleanor sent the “Signed SOW and revised schedule” email.
Confirm that her compromised account was reset and existing sessions/tokens were revoked following the compromise.
Continue monitoring ewalsh.ext and known Bexley IOCs for additional suspicious activity.
Reopen/escalate if Eleanor denies sending the message, containment cannot be confirmed, or additional malicious activity is discovered.

## Justification
The reported email is assessed as False Positive / Informational because the message itself strongly matches legitimate Bexley business communication. The sender and Return-Path match the established contact, the message followed the normal Bexley mail path, SPF/DKIM/DMARC all passed, the business context was consistent with the ongoing relationship, and the PDF contained no identified active or malicious content.

The previous compromise was specifically considered rather than assuming the message was legitimate based solely on email authentication. Searches of available audit and sign-in telemetry found no successful attacker authentication or follow-on activity surrounding the message. A known malicious IP attempted to access Eleanor's Cloudora guest account at 04:44 UTC, but Conditional Access blocked the attempt.

A remaining evidence gap is that neither CloudoraAudit_CL nor the Week 2 helpdesk records confirm Eleanor's account reset/session revocation. This should be verified directly with Eleanor/Bexley. Based on the evidence currently available, however, no malicious activity was identified in this specific reported email.

## Tuning note
No tuning recommended. Given the active Bexley compromise and the sender's previous compromise, reporting the message for review was appropriate. Similar messages from previously compromised contacts should continue to receive scrutiny rather than being suppressed based solely on sender reputation or successful SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication.
