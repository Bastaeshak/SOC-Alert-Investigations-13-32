## Alert

An unauthorized actor may have added tara.kemp to Payroll-Admins to obtain or provide unauthorized privileged access to payroll resources.

Benign hypothesis:
The group membership change may be an authorized role change associated with Tara's responsibilities and the Bexley integration.

- HD-5170, dated 2026-10-09 08:45 UTC, states:
  "Role change: tara.kemp to Payroll-Admins per integration RACI; approved change HD ref CHG-2105"

- HD-5170 therefore specifically identifies tara.kemp, the Payroll-Admins group, the integration RACI, and an approved change reference of CHG-2105.

- The documented role change matches the user and privileged group identified by CLD-0219.

- No evidence reviewed during this investigation indicates that the group membership change was unauthorized.

## Action
Close as authorized administrative activity. No containment or escalation required.

Retain the HD-5170 and CHG-2105 references with the incident for audit traceability. Re-raise if Payroll-Admins membership changes occur without an associated approved request/change or involve users outside the approved RACI.
