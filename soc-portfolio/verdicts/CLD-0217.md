# CLD-0217: Off-hours upload to young domain: LDN-SRV-APP1 to bexley-sync.example

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 5 · 2026-08-30
Verdict: Escalate · Severity (my rating): High (reported Medium)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
CLD-0217 - Off-hours upload to young domain

Rule "Data volume to newly observed domain" fired after LDN-SRV-APP1, the application server hosting the payroll integration middleware, uploaded 41 MB to bexley-sync.example (192.0.2.150) between 02:41 and 03:20 UTC through 214 POST requests.

The destination domain was first observed in tenant traffic this week and was registered six days ago. The documented legitimate Bexley feed uses SFTP to transfer.bexleypay.example.

## Hypothesis
Malicious hypothesis:
LDN-SRV-APP1 may be compromised and transferring data to attacker-controlled infrastructure designed to imitate the legitimate Bexley service.

Benign hypothesis:
The traffic could be part of a legitimate Bexley integration or another authorized payroll-related process that has not yet been identified.

## Evidence checked
- Reviewed proxy history for LDN-SRV-APP1.

- Confirmed 214 POST requests to bexley-sync.example (192.0.2.150) between 02:41 and 03:20 UTC totaling approximately 41 MB.

- Compared the activity with the documented legitimate Bexley feed. Legitimate traffic uses SFTP to transfer.bexleypay.example.

- Confirmed legitimate SFTP traffic from LDN-SRV-APP1 to transfer.bexleypay.example at 03:00 UTC during the documented schedule.

- Confirmed the suspicious POST traffic to bexley-sync.example was separate from and overlapped the legitimate Bexley SFTP transfer.

- The suspicious domain was registered six days ago, matching current threat intelligence regarding recently registered supplier-lookalike staging infrastructure.

- Scoped bexley-sync.example and 192.0.2.150 across available proxy telemetry. No other Cloudora hosts were observed communicating with this infrastructure.

- Proxy telemetry confirms 41 MB left LDN-SRV-APP1 but does not establish the contents of the transferred data.

## Action
Immediately escalate CLD-0217 to the vCISO for containment and investigation of LDN-SRV-APP1.

Recommend isolating LDN-SRV-APP1 as appropriate while preserving evidence, blocking bexley-sync.example and 192.0.2.150, and investigating the process or service responsible for the 214 POST requests.

Determine what data was contained in the 41 MB transfer and review the server for additional persistence or unauthorized activity. Scope the suspicious domain and IP across available telemetry and continue monitoring for related infrastructure.

## Justification
Proxy telemetry was reviewed to baseline LDN-SRV-APP1 and compare the suspicious transfer against its documented Bexley activity.

Query 1:
proxy
| where host == "LDN-SRV-APP1"
| order by timestamp asc

Results confirmed:
- 02:41:03 UTC - POST to bexley-sync.example (192.0.2.150), first of 214 requests.
- 02:55:17 UTC - Continued POST activity to bexley-sync.example.
- 03:00:00 UTC - SFTP to transfer.bexleypay.example (198.51.100.90), documented legitimate feed.
- 03:20:41 UTC - Final POST to bexley-sync.example, with approximately 41 MB transferred across 214 requests.

The legitimate Bexley feed therefore occurred separately from the suspicious activity. The documented feed used SFTP to transfer.bexleypay.example, while the suspicious activity used POST requests to bexley-sync.example, a domain registered six days ago.

Query 2:
proxy
| where dest_domain == "bexley-sync.example"
    or dest_ip == "192.0.2.150"
| order by timestamp asc

Results showed only LDN-SRV-APP1 communicating with bexley-sync.example / 192.0.2.150 in the available proxy telemetry. No additional Cloudora hosts were identified communicating with this infrastructure.

Verdict: Escalate.
Severity: High.

Escalation is required because a confirmed 41 MB outbound transfer occurred from the payroll integration middleware server to unauthorized, recently registered Bexley-lookalike infrastructure, and the contents and full impact of that transfer cannot be bounded with the available evidence.

High severity reflects the potential impact to the payroll integration environment combined with high confidence that the unauthorized transfer occurred. The available evidence does not confirm that the 41 MB contained payroll data, so payroll-data exfiltration is not claimed as a confirmed fact.

## Tuning note
No tuning recommended. The rule correctly identified a high-volume transfer to a newly observed domain. Continue alerting on significant outbound transfers to newly observed or recently registered domains, especially from systems handling sensitive payroll or client data.
