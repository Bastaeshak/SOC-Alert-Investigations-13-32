# CLD-0218: Sign-in from new country: omar.farah@cloudora.io (Dubai)

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 5 · 2026-08-30
Verdict: False positive · Severity (my rating): Informational (reported Medium)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
CLD-0218 - Sign-in from new country

Rule "Sign-in from new location" fired after omar.farah@cloudora.io successfully signed in from Dubai (198.51.100.201) at 06:40 UTC. The authentication succeeded on the first attempt, MFA was satisfied, and a registered Windows laptop was used.

## Hypothesis
Malicious hypothesis:
An unauthorized user may have obtained Omar's credentials and successfully authenticated from Dubai.

Benign hypothesis:
The sign-in may be legitimate activity from Omar during his documented monthly client visit to Dubai.

## Evidence checked


- Reviewed the Shift 5 morning handover. It states that omar.farah@cloudora.io is on his regular monthly client visit to Dubai this week, documented under HD-5177 and the travel calendar.

- Reviewed the helpdesk context referenced in the handover. HD-5177 documents Omar's Dubai visit, providing an expected business reason for authentication from Dubai.

- Reviewed raw sign-in telemetry for omar.farah@cloudora.io to verify that the actual activity matched the documented travel context.

- Confirmed a successful sign-in at 06:40:12 UTC from 198.51.100.201 in Dubai, AE.

- The sign-in occurred from Omar's registered Windows device, succeeded on the first attempt, and MFA was satisfied.

- Omar's sign-in baseline also shows a recurring monthly Dubai pattern, with previous Dubai activity noted on 12 Sep, 14 Aug, and 11 Jul.

- The raw authentication evidence is consistent with the Shift 5 handover, HD-5177, the travel calendar, and Omar's established historical behavior. No conflicting evidence was identified.

## Action
Close as legitimate user activity. No containment or escalation required.

Continue normal monitoring and re-raise if future Dubai activity falls outside documented travel, uses an unfamiliar device, fails MFA, or otherwise deviates from Omar's established pattern.

## Justification
Reviewed Omar Farah's authentication baseline using:

The Shift 5 handover states that Omar is currently on his regular monthly client visit to Dubai under HD-5177 and the travel calendar.

Raw sign-in evidence matches the documented context: correct user and location, registered device, MFA satisfied, and an established monthly pattern of Dubai sign-ins.

Verdict is False Positive with Informational severity because the underlying authentication activity is consistent with legitimate, documented user behavior and no evidence of malicious activity was identified.

## Tuning note
Consider narrowly suppressing or reducing priority for new-location alerts when the location matches documented travel and the authentication uses a registered device with satisfied MFA. Re-raise on deviations such as unfamiliar devices, failed MFA, unexpected locations, or activity outside the documented travel period.
