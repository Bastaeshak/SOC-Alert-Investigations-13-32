# CLD-0232: Reported: invoice emails not arriving, marcus.oje@cloudora.io

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 8 · 2026-09-04
Verdict: True positive · Severity (my rating): Medium (reported Medium)
QA review: Satisfactory

## Alert
Marcus Oje reported that invoice emails colleagues confirmed they sent had not arrived since last week. Current telemetry shows a stealth inbox rule named " " was created in his mailbox at 2026-10-08 23:57:12 UTC from known malicious IP 192.0.2.146. The rule moves messages containing “invoice” or “remittance” to RSS Subscriptions and marks them read. Marcus was not included in Monday's mailbox remediation.

## Hypothesis
Malicious: Marcus's account was compromised by the Bexley campaign operator, who created a stealth inbox rule to hide finance-related communications. The rule survived Monday's remediation because cleanup was scoped to Daniel Reeve's mailbox.

Benign: The rule was legitimately created by Marcus or an authorized process, and the missing invoice emails are unrelated to the known Bexley campaign.

## Evidence checked
Marcus's normal baseline showed a successful session on 2026-10-08 08:20:47 UTC from 203.0.113.10, London UK, using his registered Windows device. At 23:57:12 UTC, a session from 192.0.2.146, an unknown hosting IP associated with the active Bexley campaign, created the " " inbox rule through OWA.

A database-wide search identified a closely matching persistence technique against Daniel Reeve: at 2026-10-10 05:58:44 UTC, the same 192.0.2.146 infrastructure created Daniel's stealth rule "." through OWA, tracked under CLD-0221. The older August CloudoraAudit_CL result was excluded from the current Week 2 evidence.

Marcus's helpdesk report independently confirms ongoing impact: expected invoice messages have been missing since last week. Monday's remediation removed Daniel's malicious mailbox rule but did not include Marcus. Separate CLD-0231 investigation also established that Marcus's pre-reset refresh token survived Monday because session revocation was not applied to his account.

## Action
Contain Marcus's account by ensuring his credentials are reset, revoking all active sessions and refresh tokens, and removing the malicious " " inbox rule. Review his mailbox for additional malicious rules or persistence and determine whether hidden invoice/remittance messages can be recovered and whether any additional mailbox activity occurred during the compromise window. Because this finding proves Monday's remediation missed another compromised mailbox, review the full incident scope rather than limiting remediation to Marcus and Daniel. Flag the remediation gap to the vCISO and correlate CLD-0232 with the existing Bexley incident. Re-raise and formally escalate if investigation identifies client/payroll data access, additional affected hosts/sites, or malicious activity that cannot be bounded.

## Justification
CLD-0232 is a True Positive / Medium because the evidence confirms successful malicious activity inside Marcus Oje's account. At 2026-10-08 23:57:12 UTC, known Bexley operator IP 192.0.2.146 maintained an OWA session as marcus.oje@cloudora.io and created a stealth inbox rule. This does not match Marcus's established baseline of 203.0.113.10, London UK, registered Windows device.

The attribution is strengthened by the same IP creating Daniel Reeve's similar stealth inbox rule on 2026-10-10 05:58:44 UTC. Marcus's helpdesk report provides evidence of actual business impact because legitimate invoice emails were hidden by the rule. The persistence also survived Monday's remediation because Marcus was outside the mailbox-remediation scope.

Medium reflects confirmed account compromise, attacker-created mailbox persistence, ongoing finance-related business impact, and failed remediation scope. Formal Escalate is not currently required because Marcus is not identified as an executive, no client/payroll-data access has been confirmed from this alert, and the malicious activity can presently be bounded.
