# CLD-0230: Large outbound transfer from new host: LDN-SRV-APP2

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 8 · 2026-09-04
Verdict: False positive · Severity (my rating): Informational (reported Medium)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
The rule “Data volume to external destination” fired after LDN-SRV-APP2 transferred approximately 96 MB to transfer.bexleypay.example (198.51.100.90) over SFTP between 2026-10-14 01:04:12 UTC and 01:41:39 UTC.

## Hypothesis
Malicious: The replacement payroll middleware may have been compromised or may be exfiltrating data to an external destination, potentially indicating attacker activity carried over from LDN-SRV-APP1.

Benign: The transfer may be the authorized first-run backlog transfer from the newly built replacement payroll server under CHG-2110, with the unusually high volume caused by several days of accumulated payroll data.

## Evidence checked
Proxy telemetry for LDN-SRV-APP2 showed two SFTP transfers to transfer.bexleypay.example (198.51.100.90) on 2026-10-14 at 01:04:12 UTC and 01:41:39 UTC, totaling approximately 96 MB. Both events are marked as the first two backlog transfers associated with CHG-2110. A broader destination search showed that the previous payroll server, LDN-SRV-APP1, had also used the same domain, IP, and SFTP protocol for its documented legitimate payroll feed on 2026-10-09 03:00 UTC. APP2 later contacted the same destination on 2026-10-16 06:00:11 UTC, transferring approximately 4 MB during the documented scheduled feed window.

Helpdesk record HD-5205, opened 2026-10-13 09:30 UTC, independently confirms that CHG-2110 authorized the build of LDN-SRV-APP2 as the replacement payroll middleware and specifically states that a backlog transfer to transfer.bexleypay.example was expected on the first run. HD-5201 also documents that APP1 had been seized for forensics and the payroll middleware was offline pending replacement.

The activity therefore matches the documented host, destination, purpose, protocol, and expected first-run behavior.

## Action
Close the alert as expected activity under CHG-2110 and reference HD-5205 in the ticket. No containment is required. Continue normal monitoring of LDN-SRV-APP2; re-raise if future transfers occur to a different destination or IP, use an unexpected protocol, fall outside the documented payroll schedule/change activity, or show unexplained volume inconsistent with the normal payroll feed.

## Justification
The underlying activity is legitimate and authorized rather than malicious. The alert triggered because APP2 was a newly built host with no established outbound baseline and its first run transferred several days of accumulated payroll backlog. Raw proxy evidence confirms that APP2 sent the data only to the established payroll destination transfer.bexleypay.example (198.51.100.90) over SFTP. The previous payroll server APP1 used the same destination and protocol, and APP2 subsequently settled into a smaller scheduled transfer pattern. Most importantly, HD-5205 independently documents before the alert that APP2 was built under CHG-2110 and that a backlog transfer to this exact destination was expected on its first run. This rules out the malicious hypothesis with the available evidence and supports False Positive / Informational.

Another analyst can retrace the network checks with:

proxy
| where host == "LDN-SRV-APP2"
| order by timestamp asc

and:

proxy
| where dest_domain == "transfer.bexleypay.example"
| order by timestamp asc

## Tuning note
Do not disable the data-volume rule globally because large outbound transfers remain valuable for detecting exfiltration. Instead, baseline the newly introduced LDN-SRV-APP2 payroll feed and correlate future alerts with the expected combination of host LDN-SRV-APP2, destination transfer.bexleypay.example / 198.51.100.90, SFTP protocol, and documented payroll schedule/change windows.
