# CLD-0221: Inbox rule created on daniel.reeve: external forward of finance mail

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 6 · 2026-08-31
Verdict: Escalate · Severity (my rating): High (reported High)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
Suspicious inbox rule created on daniel.reeve@cloudora.io at 05:58 UTC Saturday. Rule named . forwards messages containing invoice, payment, or Bexley to billing@bexleypay-billing.example and marks them as read. Rule was created through OWA from 192.0.2.146, a standing watchlist IP.

## Hypothesis
Malicious hypothesis: An attacker obtained authenticated access to Daniel Reeve's mailbox and created a forwarding rule for finance/Bexley-related messages to maintain visibility into sensitive business communications while hiding forwarded messages from the user.

Benign hypothesis: Daniel intentionally created the forwarding rule for a legitimate business purpose. However, the source IP is a known watchlist IOC and the external forwarding destination is not established as authorized in the available context.

## Evidence checked
Alert details and Shift 6 morning handover/watchlist standing order. Confirmed the mailbox action originated from 192.0.2.146. The handover states that any successful authentication involving 192.0.2.146 or 192.0.2.203 requires immediate phone escalation before completing the investigation.

## Action
Immediately escalate to the vCISO by phone per standing order. Request containment of Daniel's account/session and preservation of mailbox evidence. After escalation, investigate the authentication/session, determine how access was obtained, identify messages matched or forwarded by the rule, remove the malicious rule, revoke active sessions as appropriate, and scope 192.0.2.146 and the external forwarding destination across available telemetry.

## Justification
Confirmed account compromise resulted in successful authenticated mailbox access and modification from standing watchlist IP 192.0.2.146. The attacker successfully created a concealed external forwarding rule specifically targeting invoice, payment, and Bexley communications and marking matching messages as read. This demonstrates successful access, mailbox manipulation, targeted knowledge of Cloudora's current business activity, and the capability to collect sensitive communications externally. The activity also occurred during the unstaffed weekend period, increasing potential dwell time. Actual messages forwarded and resulting data exposure have not yet been established and require further investigation.

## Tuning note
None at this stage - the detection identified behavior requiring immediate escalation.
